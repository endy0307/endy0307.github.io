Digital Traveler, Physical World

In a society steered by technology, people have begun jumping into the Metaverse to reflect upon the future progressions of our reality and the virtual world. The internet offers vicarious satisfaction; as a result, digital smog and artificial senses have become mainstream.

To enhance the experience of the digital traveler on the move, the design focuses on mobility, durability, agility, and comfort. A variety of technical materials, including breathable and wear-resistant fabrics, are selected to allow digital travelers to shuttle seamlessly between different metropolises and terrains.
