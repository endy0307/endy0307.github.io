Connect x Hazzys — Hazzys Football Club
A design competition project for Connect x Hazzys, themed around the creation of Hazzys Football Club (HFC). The collection bridges British heritage sartorial elegance with the spirit and energy of modern football culture.

Sartorial Match

"Sartorial Match" explores the evolution from Hazzys Royal Club (HRC) to Hazzys Football Club (HFC), connecting British heritage with the spirit of modern play.

It reflects two meanings of match: the perfect harmony between sartorial elegance and football energy, and the match as the game itself — a symbol of unity and play.

The project extends HRC into HFC, establishing a new visual identity through the creation of the HFC monogram and emblem. The look blends structured elegance with vintage athletic charm.

Fireman Clip hardware, the embroidered coat of arms, and custom jacquard fabric were developed from original designs, highlighting craftsmanship and authenticity while maintaining a relaxed, contemporary tone.

Sartorial Match unites the athletic ease of a football jersey with the refined codes of semi-formal dressing — achieving a perfect balance between sport and style.
