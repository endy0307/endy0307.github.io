Material Realism & Cinematic Rendering

A visual study in material realism and cinematic rendering. My goal was to replicate the high-impact aesthetic of a fashion campaign image through meticulous texture tuning and light play.

By refining PBR maps in CLO3D to capture the nuances of heavy-wash denim and utilizing advanced lighting in Blender, I created a photorealistic scene that explores the boundaries of digital fabric expression.
